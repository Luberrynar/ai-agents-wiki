# Examples of AI agents
**Summary:** Agents are used in coding, customer support, research and many business tasks. IBM also gives simple everyday examples for each type of agent.
**Key points:**
- The Wikipedia article describes seven kinds of agents: business-task, conversational, research, analytics, coding, domain-specific and web browser agents. (Source: source-01)
- By August 2025 a magazine called software development the most definitive use of AI agents, and by October 2025 coding agents and customer support were the main business uses. (Source: source-01)
- Anthropic highlights customer support and coding as good fits because they have clear success measures and feedback loops. (Source: source-02)
- Anthropic's coding agent can resolve real GitHub issues from a task description, and its computer-use demo lets the model operate a computer. (Source: source-02)
- IBM's five agent types, simplest to most advanced: simple reflex (a thermostat that turns on heating at a set time), model-based reflex (a robot vacuum), goal-based (a navigation system choosing the fastest route), utility-based (a route balancing fuel, time and tolls) and learning (product recommendations on shopping sites). (Source: source-03)
- IBM's walkthrough has an agent plan a surfing trip in Greece by gathering weather data and asking a surfing-specialist agent. (Source: source-03)
- IBM lists use areas including customer experience, healthcare, emergency response, and finance and supply chain. (Source: source-03)
